Cloudflare dashboard recovery:
1. Workers & Pages > schoolyard-buddies-multiplayer-test.
2. Deployments: confirm there is an active successful deployment. Roll back to the last successful version if the latest build failed.
3. Settings > Domains & Routes. Enable the workers.dev route.
4. Confirm the public URL is exactly the current Worker name plus your account subdomain.
5. Open the public URL in a private browser window before testing the PWA.
6. Only after the URL opens, remove/reinstall the iPhone PWA to clear the cached offline copy.
